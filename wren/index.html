a quiet, warm place to browse.

wren is a browser that feels like a lamp left on in the next room. nothing is sent home on its own, and nothing follows you around.

download for windowsbrowse add-ons

what it does

a short, honest list. everything here works today.

answers in the address bar

type a sum, a word or a place and the answer shows up before you press enter. it comes from nook, and nothing is stored.

a privacy page that shows its work

see how many trackers and cookies wren turned away today, and which sites tried.

warm all day

the window slowly shifts with the time of day, and each site can tint it a little. bedtime mode takes the blue out of the evening.

room to think

focus mode hides the bars. workspaces keep projects apart. tabs you forget about go to sleep. a reading view cleans up long pages.

small sounds

rain, a fire, a cafe or waves, made softly on your computer. the welcome has a music-box tune you can mute.

mods

a small file restyles the whole window. add one from the add-ons page and take it off when you like.

firefox underneath

wren is built on firefox, so it renders the web the way you expect, and runs the extensions you already use.

install

one file, no administrator rights. it installs for your account and can be removed from windows settings.

download wren-setup.exe
  1. 1
    download and open the file. wren is not code-signed yet, so windows may show a blue smartscreen box. choose more info, then run anyway.
  2. 2
    let the egg hatch. the installer puts wren in %localappdata%\Programs\Wren and adds a start menu shortcut. nothing else changes.
  3. 3
    say hello. on first run wren can bring over bookmarks, history and extensions from a firefox profile on this computer.

to check the download, its sha-256 is 7a9b1f957c7c1438579dd42b67441b571845f015764245f8cb8e5c739dc3438d

wren checks for newer versions by itself and asks before it updates. the source is on github.

what it does not do (yet)

wren has no accounts, so there is no sync between your computers. it is windows-only for now, and mac and linux builds have not been made.
