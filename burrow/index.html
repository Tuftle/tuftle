a quiet place for your passwords.

burrow keeps your logins in one scrambled file that only your master password can open. there are no accounts, and nothing is sent anywhere unless you turn it on.

download for windowshow it keeps you safe

what it does

a short, honest list. everything here works today.

one scrambled file

your logins are encrypted on your computer before they are saved. the file is useless without your master password.

fills logins in wren

open a login page and a small chip offers the right login. it only ever appears for that exact site, and only after you click.

a generator you can remember

random characters, or seven plain words like otter-lantern-moss-quill-heron-brook-sage. each word adds eight bits.

one-time codes

paste the setup key from a site and burrow shows the six digit code, counting down.

a checkup

finds weak and reused passwords on your computer. an optional breach check sends only five characters of a fingerprint, never a password.

bring yours, take yours

import from chrome, edge, firefox or bitwarden. export an encrypted backup any time.

how it keeps you safe

in plain words. the longer version is in the threat model.

your master passwordnever saved and never sent. it is stretched with argon2id, a deliberately slow function, so guessing it is expensive.
your loginsencrypted with aes-256-gcm. a changed or damaged file is refused instead of opened.
if you forget the passwordthe recovery key you saved opens the vault. without both, nobody can, including us. there is no reset.
the clipboardcopied passwords are cleared after twenty seconds.
lockingafter five quiet minutes, or at once with the lock button or ctrl+l. in wren, the panic key locks it too.
what it cannot doprotect a computer that already has a keylogger on it, or a master password that is easy to guess.

install

pick whichever suits you. both are one file and need no administrator rights.

burrow for windows windows 10 and 11, 64-bit · about 6 MB
download burrow-setup.exe
  1. 1
    burrow-setup.exe installs for your account, adds a start menu shortcut, and can be removed from windows settings. an acorn walks into its burrow while it works.
  2. 2
    burrow-portable.exe is the same app in one file. put it on a usb stick and your vault stays next to it, in a burrow-data folder.
  3. 3
    in wren burrow is already built in. press alt+shift+v to open it. in firefox and chrome there is an extension that fills logins the same way. it is not in the add-on stores yet, so you load it yourself, here is how.

burrow is not code-signed yet, so windows may show a blue smartscreen box. choose more info, then run anyway. to check the download, the sha-256 of burrow-setup.exe is 190ab6fa3f6b03e039856b3c07e7cb896630c4435ccddba4dc04c2cfeae49d38 and of burrow-portable.exe 6d2530fa309e967ea195e34eaab5b9f81bd8ae8f5bb85bc31d6d448d4a42ce4a.

the source is on github.

what it does not do (yet)

burrow has no sync between your computers yet, and the apps are windows only for now. you can copy the encrypted backup between machines, or keep the vault file in a folder you already sync yourself.
